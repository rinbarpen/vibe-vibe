# Figure brief — 09_stat_single

- Theme: portable river-water turbidity monitoring.
- Figure type: single-panel calibration-style response plot.
- Data status: **fully synthetic simulated example**, saved in `data.csv`; five replicates at each nominal turbidity standard level.
- X: nominal turbidity standard (NTU). NTU denotes turbidity, not mass concentration. Y: normalized transmission (arbitrary units).
- Display raw observations and mean ± sample SD only. No fit, significance, detection limit, or inference is claimed.
- Omnidraw route: new data chart → Matplotlib. NaturePanelForge panel-to-code is for reconstructing an existing image, which is absent here.
